## 7 Top Stocks to Buy and Hold for the Next Decade and Beyond

**Source:** Lyn Alden - Investment Strategy

**Key points:**

- Enterprise Products Partners (EPD): Diversified midstream pipeline operator with 25 years of distribution growth, 7.5%+ yield, wide moat from pipeline infrastructure, and strong balance sheet.
- Brookfield Corporation (BN): Global asset manager with $850B+ AUM in real assets; contrarian management buys distressed assets cheaply and recycles capital, giving it a wide moat and strong growth.

- HDFC Bank (HDB), Canadian Natural Resources (CNQ), CVS Health (CVS), and Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) round out the list, chosen for economic moats, strong balance sheets, and reasonable valuations for long-term holding.

## Quality stocks for uncertain times

**Source:** Fidelity Investments

**Key points:**

- Fidelity fund managers favor high-quality companies with strong brands, competitive moats, and recurring/subscription revenues to navigate 2026 market uncertainty, citing Costco and Verisk Analytics as examples of predictable 'tollbooth' businesses.
- Amazon is highlighted for its 'annuity-type' Prime subscription fees plus e-commerce and AWS cloud growth, described as 'an online Costco, but also with a cloud business.'
- Big tech names like NVIDIA, Alphabet, and Meta Platforms are cited as blue-chip/growth hybrids benefiting from self-reinforcing 'flywheel' network effects, having outgrown the market for 15+ years.
- Netflix and Roblox are noted for recurring revenue and 'hit-driven' flywheel dynamics, while Descartes Systems and Veeva Systems are flagged as undervalued quality picks in software and health care sectors hit by temporary sentiment-driven selloffs.

**Link:** https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/investing-in-quality
